Cooking Classes at La Belle Vie

Learn from our expert chefs

March 15, 2026 Chef Team 4 min read Classes

Master the Art of Cooking

Ever wondered how our chefs create such incredible dishes? Now you can learn from them! We offer cooking classes for all skill levels, from beginners to experienced home cooks.

"Cooking is not just about following recipes—it's about understanding ingredients, techniques, and the joy of creating something beautiful."

— Chef Antonio Rossi

Our classes include a variety of cuisines and techniques to suit every interest:

  • 🍳 Italian Pasta Making: Learn to make fresh pasta from scratch
  • 🥖 French Pastry: Master the art of croissants and macarons
  • 🍣 Sushi Workshop: Roll like a pro with our sushi chef
  • 🍷 Wine Tasting: Discover the world of wine with our sommelier
  • 🌿 Farm-to-Table: Cook with seasonal ingredients from our garden
  • 🔥 Grilling Masterclass: Perfect your grilling techniques

Class Details: Classes are held every Saturday morning from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM. All ingredients, recipes, and a meal at the end are included.

Each class is limited to 12 participants to ensure personalized attention. You'll receive a recipe booklet to take home, and you'll enjoy the meal you've prepared at the end of the class.

Whether you're looking to impress your friends with homemade pasta or simply want to learn new culinary skills, our cooking classes offer a fun and educational experience for everyone.

Book your spot today! Contact us to reserve your place in our next cooking class.
